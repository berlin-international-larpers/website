Berlin International Larping

Berlin International Larping brings together English speaking larpers based in or visiting Berlin, in order to larp together in the city and coordinate trips to international larps.

We are a queer friendly, inclusive space.

Our Upcoming Larps in Berlin

The locations for all our larps is a collaborative space in Wedding. In order to cover the rental costs, participation costs 5euro which should be payed in advance.

The List

    • 2026 October 25 - Think of Children - Sign Ups Open Until September 27

      A nontransparent dark comedy chamber larp about the things children do and the excuses their parents make for them It is presented by a GM who also moderates the discussion as the teacher and lasts around 3 hours including workshops and debrief. The larp takes place entirely in the kindergarten classroom and all players portray the parent of a child (3-6 years old) who was involved; character creation is part of the workshop.
